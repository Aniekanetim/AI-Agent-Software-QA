# Business rules

**Maintenance:** Human business/product owner. Agents read applicable rules and propose clarifications; observed behavior does not populate this baseline automatically.

**Owner:** not assigned. **State:** template; no rules supplied. **Applicable revision/date:** not supplied.

## Canonical rules

Use stable IDs such as BR-001. Define calculations, eligibility, boundaries, units, timezones, identity, permissions and recovery only where they apply to the real product. Requirements come from the responsible owner or supplied authority, not the current application response.

## Assumptions and changes

State unresolved assumptions, dependencies and operational limits explicitly. Link [feature acceptance criteria](../product/PRD.md) rather than maintaining a second feature specification here. An accepted change needs an owner, reason, applicable revision and linked decision; preserve the previous baseline for earlier results.

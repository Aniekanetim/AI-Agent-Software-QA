## Define the approach

State business goals, critical journeys, quality objectives, constraints, available skills/tools and responsibilities. Explain risk assessment and prioritization, applicable test levels and interfaces, design techniques, data/environment needs, entry/exit criteria and evidence retention. Distinguish representative sampling from exhaustive evaluation. Refer to [design guidance](../Test%20Design%20and%20Exploration/README.md) and the project's [coverage definitions](../Coverage%20and%20Traceability/README.md).

## Maintain the risk register

| Risk ID | Failure and impact | Likelihood / impact / priority rationale | Linked source and revision | Mitigating condition / test | Owner / remaining exposure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Choose a project-appropriate risk scale and explain it. Record an unknown rather than fabricate precision. A risk can justify investigation without becoming an approved product requirement. Missing intended behavior still needs an owner decision before dependent correctness judgments.

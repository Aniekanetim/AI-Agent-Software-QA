## Briefing supplied

Summarize the objective, itemized checks/claims, expected outcomes or evidence, rationale, alternatives, uncertainty, limits and exact next authorization. The conversational request itself must contain essential context; a link here cannot replace it.

## Focused confirmation for G3

Supply exact preconditions, inputs, numbered actions and observable checkpoints to confirm the finding. Include the cause/evidence check, severity impact, priority urgency, current defect state and corrective approach. Ask for observations and an approve/reject/change/investigate/blocked response. The human need not replay the entire agent suite.

## Actual response and review method

Not received. When supplied, record the human's actual reply and date, what they reviewed or reproduced, observations, conditions and unresolved limits. If the method was not supplied, say so. A brief approval is not evidence of unreported manual testing or source inspection.

## Consequences and next checkpoint

Record exactly what is now authorized and what remains unapproved. Findings acceptance does not authorize fixed execution; a local QA handoff is not production release authority. Record waiting state and avoid duplicate unanswered requests.

For G5, link the prepared [release-notes/sign-off/handoff packet](completion-handoff.md). Identify the target, exit-criteria evidence, accepted conditions, next-owner actions, authorized delivery and any required receipt. Record the actual decision first, then update delivery/receipt from observations. Do not claim that publication establishes another owner's acceptance or close required handoff work while it remains pending.
